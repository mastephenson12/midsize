Privacy Policy

This page explains important rules and practices for using MidSize AI. Please read it carefully.

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect contact details, company and project information, messages, form submissions, appointment requests, marketing preferences, billing-related records, device and browser information, usage events, and information you choose to provide in a roofing estimate or other document.

Roofing estimate and document tools

Some estimate-review tools may process files locally in your browser. Other features, such as requesting a human follow-up or sending information to an AI-powered service, may transmit information to our systems or service providers. Review the notice shown at the specific tool before uploading. Remove unnecessary personal, financial, or sensitive information before submitting a document. We do not promise that every tool operates only on your device.

We use information to respond to requests, provide and improve tools, manage customer relationships, schedule services, operate authorized automations, prevent abuse, secure the website, process transactions where applicable, and send communications consistent with your choices and applicable law.

Our services may use AI to analyze user-provided information, generate educational output, answer inquiries, or assist with customer service. AI outputs may be inaccurate. Where enabled and lawful, communications may be handled by automated systems. Consent to receive marketing texts is not required to purchase services. Opt-out instructions are provided with applicable communications.

We may use cookies, similar technologies, analytics, and measurement tools to understand site performance and improve services. Browser controls may let you restrict cookies. Where required, we will request consent before using nonessential tracking technologies.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect rights and safety, or in a business transaction. We do not describe this as a guarantee that information is never shared for advertising purposes; applicable opt-out rights are addressed below.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, subject to legal obligations and operational needs. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to opt out of marketing. Depending on your state or country, you may have additional rights, including rights concerning targeted advertising, sale or sharing of personal information, and appeals. We will verify requests where required and respond under applicable law. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the message and opt out of eligible texts by replying STOP.
