2. Choose the right message

Replace the bracketed details and review the record before sending through your usual channel.

Confirm receipt

Hi [First name], this is [Name] with [Company]. Did the estimate for [project] come through and open correctly? I’m happy to explain anything in the scope or next steps.

Answer a useful question

Hi [First name], is there anything in the estimate for [project] you would like us to clarify? I can walk through what is included, the options, or the next steps so you can make an informed decision.

Close the loop

Hi [First name], I’m closing the loop on the estimate for [project]. Is the project still being considered, postponed, or no longer needed? If you prefer no further follow-up, let me know and I’ll update our records.

3. Agree on a simple schedule

  • When sent: record the estimate and agree on the next contact.
  • Around two business days later: confirm receipt if still unconfirmed.
  • Around days 5–7: offer to clarify a relevant question.
  • Around days 10–14: ask for the current status and close the loop.

This is an example, not a required sequence. Follow the customer’s requested timing and contact preferences. Stop on request; pause for replies, revisions, or active conversations.

4. Check the process with your team

  • Every active estimate has an owner and dated next action.
  • The team checks replies before sending.
  • Scope, price, and technical questions go to a qualified person.
  • Declines and stop requests are recorded and respected.
  • Weekly review counts contacts, replies, decisions, and overdue actions.
  • Booked value and collected revenue are recorded separately.
