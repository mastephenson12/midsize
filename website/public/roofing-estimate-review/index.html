How do you read a roofing estimate before signing?

Compare the written work, materials, exclusions, extra-charge rules, warranties and payment milestones before comparing totals. A topic being mentioned is a starting point for questions, not proof that the work or terms are adequate.

By Mark Stephenson, founder of MidSize AI · Published November 13, 2026

This is a fictional teaching example, not a customer estimate, market-price benchmark, contractor recommendation or customer success story. The numbers are illustrative. This walkthrough uses the same sample text available in our Estimate Decoder; the annotations below are editorial explanations, not a claim about an automated result.

The sample roofing proposal

Remove one layer of existing asphalt shingles down to roof decking. Replace damaged decking at $95 per sheet with owner approval. Install synthetic underlayment, architectural shingles, starter, ridge cap, new drip edge and pipe flashings. Install ridge ventilation where applicable. Contractor will obtain required permit and haul away roofing debris. Includes 10-year workmanship warranty and manufacturer limited warranty. Total price $18,750. Deposit $1,875, balance due after final inspection. Additional structural repairs are not included and require a written change order.

What does that wording actually tell you?

Six places to ask for written clarification
Proposal wordingWhat it establishesWhat to ask next
Remove one layerThe proposal describes removal of one existing shingle layer.What happens if another layer is found? Ask how any additional work will be priced and approved.
Decking at $95 per sheetA unit price and owner-approval step are named.What sheet size and material does that price cover? Does it include labor and disposal? How will the quantity and condition be documented?
Synthetic underlayment and architectural shinglesMaterial categories are listed.Which manufacturer, product and color? Which parts of the roof are included? Request the product specifications in the written scope.
Ridge ventilation where applicableVentilation is conditional.Who decides whether it applies, using what assessment? Ask what ventilation work is included and whether it changes the price.
10-year workmanship and manufacturer limited warrantyTwo types of warranty are mentioned.Ask for both written warranties, who provides each, coverage, exclusions, registration requirements and the process for reporting a problem.
Balance after final inspection; structural repairs excludedA payment milestone and an exclusion are named.Whose inspection triggers payment? How are outstanding items handled? Ask for the written change-order process before extra work begins.

What does the total leave unanswered?

The example does not identify the measured roof area, exact products, start and completion expectations, tax treatment, or whether every accessory and permit-related charge is included. Ask the contractor to clarify these details. An omission from this short sample is not evidence of dishonest work.

You cannot determine whether $18,750 is a fair price from this text. Scope, roof geometry, access, location, materials and actual site conditions can change the work. Compare proposals for equivalent work and ask a qualified local professional about the roof itself.

A message you can send your roofer

Thanks for the estimate. Before I decide, could you send an updated written scope listing the exact materials, roof areas included, additional-layer and decking charges, ventilation decision, exclusions, timeline and payment milestones? Please include both written warranties and explain how we approve and document changes before extra work starts. I want to compare the proposals on the same basis.
