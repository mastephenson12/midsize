Questions

What roofing owners usually ask.

Does Samantha replace my office staff?

She is best used as overflow, after-hours, or consistent first-line intake. Your staff remains responsible for professional advice, inspection, pricing, and customer relationships.

Can she provide roof prices?

Only if you explicitly approve a carefully limited response. We recommend using her to gather details and book the next step instead of promising a binding price.

Can she connect to my CRM or calendar?

The intended setup includes a tailored routing workflow. Compatibility and implementation details are confirmed during the demo and onboarding process.

Will callers know they are speaking with AI?

The greeting and disclosure approach should be configured to match applicable laws and your business preferences.
