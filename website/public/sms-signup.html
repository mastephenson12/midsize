How SMS consent works

When completing a MidSize AI signup or inquiry form, you may choose whether to receive text messages. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. You must actively check the relevant unchecked consent box on the actual submission form.

Informational messages (optional): I agree to receive text messages from MidSize AI about my inquiries, appointment confirmations and reminders, estimate or service updates, and customer-care replies. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Marketing messages (separate, optional): I agree to receive promotional text messages from MidSize AI about services, offers, and updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

MidSize AI does not charge for messages; carrier message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Reply STOP to cancel; you will receive one confirmation text and no further messages unless you opt in again. Reply HELP or contact us for assistance.
