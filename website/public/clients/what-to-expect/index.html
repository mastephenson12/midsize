MidSize AIServices and pricing

Your client journey

Know what happens next.

Before work begins, agree on the service, price, responsibilities, and update schedule with Mark. This page explains the process; it is not a live project-status dashboard.

1. Inquiry and fit conversation

A submitted inquiry requests a conversation. It does not purchase a service, reserve a start date, activate an agent, or book an inspection. Keep the confirmation shown by the form. If you do not receive a response, email mark@midsizeai.com.

2. Written scope before payment

Your written proposal should identify deliverables, setup and recurring fees, included usage, overage rates, possible third-party charges, support boundaries, cancellation terms, and any minimum commitment. Confirm how extra spending is authorized and what happens at a usage limit. Starting prices on the website are not a final quote.

Agree on the start date, what your team must supply, and which systems can be connected. If a required integration has not been verified, record it as pending rather than included and working.

3. Kickoff and approvals

Name an owner on each team and agree on the communication channel and next update date. Confirm the starting baseline and what success will mean. For AI call services, review scripts, booking permissions, escalation rules, and test calls before approving launch.

The $500 estimate follow-up pilot is process setup and coaching: your team sends customer messages. Automated calling, texting, custom integrations, and ongoing managed follow-up are outside that pilot.

4. Updates while work is underway

Agree on update dates at kickoff. Each update should state what is completed, what remains unverified, what is blocked, who owns the next action, and when the next update is due. A requested approval remains pending until you give it.

If something changes the scope, cost, or timeline, we explain the change and obtain your agreement before proceeding with that change. Revised dates and decisions belong in the written project record. A delay should come with an explanation and a next update date.

5. Review and handover

Review delivered items and unresolved work together. Keep inquiry counts, appointments, booked jobs, and collected revenue separate. Illustrative demos and calculator estimates are not client results. Appointments, sales, revenue, and Google rankings are not guaranteed.

For the 14-day follow-up pilot, the agreed process includes a kickoff, midpoint check-in, and closing review. Ongoing support or another service requires its own agreed scope.

Keep a useful written record

Prepare a kickoff plan or draft a project update. These tools create drafts on your device. They do not send messages, confirm approvals, or update a live project record.
