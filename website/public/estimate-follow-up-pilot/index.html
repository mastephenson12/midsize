How the 14 days work

Start small. Put it into practice. Review the results.

  1. Days 1–2 · Map the process

    Review how estimates are sent and tracked. Agree on a manageable group of open estimates, a team owner, and the starting counts.

  2. Days 3–5 · Set up the workflow

    Configure the tracker in a shared spreadsheet or your existing system, prepare messages, and walk the team through the process.

  3. Days 6–13 · Use and adjust

    Your team reviews and sends messages through its usual channels. We check in at the midpoint and help resolve process gaps.

  4. Day 14 · Review and hand over

    Compare activity and outcomes with the starting position. Leave with the tracker, approved messages, and a simple operating checklist.

A good fit if…

  • Someone on your team can own follow-up during the pilot.
