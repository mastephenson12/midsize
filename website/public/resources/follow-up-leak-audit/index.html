    Common questions

    Is a low score proof that I am losing a specific amount of revenue?

    No. The audit identifies process risk, not guaranteed lost revenue. Use the result to decide which stage should be measured more closely in your CRM, call tracking, calendar, or lead source reporting.

    Do I need AI to fix every weak area?

    No. Some leaks are best fixed with clearer ownership, a better SOP, staffing, or CRM discipline. Automation is useful where speed, consistency, after-hours coverage, or repetitive follow-up is the bottleneck.

    Does this replace my office staff or CSR?

    It should not. The useful role for automation is to handle repetitive capture, confirmation, routing, and nurture so your team can spend more time on conversations that need judgment and human context.
