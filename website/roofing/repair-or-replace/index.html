Roof basics

What type of roof do you have?

Choose the closest answer. The material changes how age and isolated damage should be interpreted.

Question 2

Approximately how old is the roof system?

If you have tile, think about the underlayment age when known—not only the visible tile.

Question 3

How widespread does the visible problem appear?

Stay on the ground. Do not climb onto the roof to answer this question.

Question 4

What is happening with leaks?

Repeated leaking can matter more than one isolated event, but only an inspection can identify the cause.

Question 5

How many roof repairs have already been attempted?

Repeated repairs in the same or multiple areas deserve a clearer written explanation of the underlying problem.

Question 6

Has a contractor documented the condition?

A recommendation should be supported with understandable evidence—not merely urgency.

Safety check

Do you see any immediate danger signs?

Examples include a sagging ceiling, water near electrical fixtures, smoke, sparks, or material actively falling.
