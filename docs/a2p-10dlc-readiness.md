## Business identity

## Website and opt-in
- [ ] Confirm public Privacy Policy and Terms pages load on mobile and are linked from every SMS opt-in form.
- [ ] Privacy Policy must explain SMS use and state that SMS opt-in data/consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Do not promise absolute non-sharing that conflicts with necessary service providers.
- [ ] Use a distinct, optional, unchecked marketing SMS checkbox. Do not bundle consent into Terms acceptance or require marketing consent to obtain services.
- [ ] If service/transactional SMS is offered, provide a separate, clearly described optional checkbox or otherwise document a valid, compliant consent mechanism for that use case.
- [ ] Show brand name, purpose, message frequency (e.g., varies), message/data rates, STOP and HELP instructions, Privacy Policy and Terms links adjacent to opt-in.
- [ ] Ensure /sms-signup, /application and any GHL-hosted forms show consistent disclosures; verify redirects and embedded forms on live URLs.
- [ ] Preserve existing homeowner promises that inquiries do not opt people into marketing.

## GHL configuration and evidence
- [ ] Confirm consent field values, opt-in timestamp, form/source URL, disclosure version and channel are retained and auditable.
- [ ] Check opt-out suppression, STOP and HELP auto-replies, and do not text numbers without the relevant consent.
- [ ] Match A2P campaign use case, opt-in workflow URL/screenshots, sample messages and opt-out/help language to actual website and GHL workflow.
- [ ] Send controlled tests for service messages, marketing messages, STOP, HELP, and opt-out persistence.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See Privacy Policy and Terms.
