# MidSize AI: customer growth messaging

## Positioning
Get Found. Get Trusted. Get Booked.

Help home-service businesses build local visibility, earn homeowner trust and turn inquiries into booked appointments. Roofing is the first implementation; retain the independent homeowner education path.

## Brand story
- Hero: the contractor who wants a steady pipeline without spending evenings chasing inquiries.
- Problem: inconsistent visibility, missed calls and incomplete follow-up.
- Empathy: good contractors should not lose opportunities just because competitors market better.
- Guide: MidSize AI, with practical workflow discovery and human handoff.
- Plan: get found through helpful content and local visibility; get trusted through real project evidence and honest reviews; get booked through clear calls to action and prompt follow-up.
- CTA: Request My Free Growth Audit (existing workflow audit application). Do not imply a comprehensive marketing assessment until delivery scope is confirmed.
- Success: more qualified opportunities and less administrative friction; no guaranteed revenue or bookings.

## Content and conversion
Each post addresses one customer problem, uses approved factual evidence, and points to a relevant service page, useful resource or inquiry form. Track source, qualified inquiries, booked appointments and attributable outcomes when available. Do not use fabricated testimonials, undisclosed referral arrangements, or unsolicited automated messages. Keep homeowner education separate from paid contractor placement and disclose any material relationships.
