# Estimate Follow-Up Pilot: delivery checklist

Offer: $500 fixed fee, 14 days from the agreed kickoff. One team and one estimate follow-up process. No automatic renewal. Confirm scope, availability, payment terms, and any cancellation arrangements in writing before collecting payment. Do not take payment until delivery capacity is confirmed.

## Fit conversation

Ask which system holds estimates, approximate open-estimate count, who follows up, what happens after a reply, and where the process gets stuck. Do not assume that a prospect loses leads. Determine whether the existing system can support a simple tracker without custom integration. Agree on a manageable pilot group; exclude contacts who requested no further communication.

## Days 1–2

- Agree on a start date and one team owner.
- Record baseline counts: estimates in scope, estimates with an owner, estimates with a next action, and known outcomes.
- Create the tracker in the contractor's existing approved workspace. Prefer estimate IDs; keep customer details in the contractor's system.
- Confirm that the contractor reviews and sends all customer messages.

## Days 3–5

- Use fields: estimate ID, assigned owner, estimate date, last contact date, status, customer question, next action, next action date, outcome, booked value, collected revenue.
- Adapt receipt-check, question-answering, and close-the-loop messages from the public builder.
- Agree on appropriate timing and stopping rules. Check for replies and contact preferences before each message.
- Train the team using fictional examples first.

## Days 6–13

Team updates the tracker after each action. Midpoint check-in: resolve unclear ownership, overdue actions, and confusing templates. This offer does not include daily outsourced outreach, automated calling/texting, custom integrations, or paid software.

## Day 14

Report raw counts for attempts, unique estimates contacted, replies, appointments, won/lost/pending decisions, booked value, and collected revenue. State the observation window and data gaps. Do not attribute every outcome to the pilot or treat booked value as cash collected.

Deliver tracker, three approved message templates, a one-page operating checklist, and the closing review. Ask separately for permission before publishing any customer quote or results. Discuss optional further work only under a new agreement.
