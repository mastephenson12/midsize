Testing and measurement
Start with three campaigns: homeowner estimate decoder, homeowner quote comparison, and roofer free kit. Add the paid pilot and missed-call review as the first pages are validated. Give each offer two or three distinct posts over an initial four-week learning period. Use post_01, post_02, etc. as utm_content and keep a record of each publication date and account. Compare audiences separately; a free worksheet and a paid pilot are different commitments.
Record platform impressions, tagged offer visits, primary CTA clicks, tool use where measurable, genuine inquiries, fit conversations, paid customers, and delivery cost. CTA clicks are navigation intent, not completed leads or purchases. A mailto click is not a sent email. Do not declare a winning offer from one post or a handful of visits. Review rates and absolute counts with audience, reach, and post format in context.

Tracking implementation
New offer pages use existing GA4 property G-GMPF16SQ8L. Events: offer_view and offer_cta_click. Parameters: offer_id, audience, campaign_source, campaign_name, post_id, cta_position. Register needed event-scoped custom dimensions and verify in GA4 before reporting. Only controlled source/post labels are sent; no estimate text, contact details, or arbitrary URL parameters. Respect browser DNT/GPC. Preserve controlled campaign labels on the next internal link. This does not yet carry attribution through every downstream form, persist cross-session attribution, or count form submissions. Confirm GHL accepts and stores mapped source fields before claiming CRM attribution.

Connecting homeowners and providers
Stage 1: give homeowners useful estimate education without requiring a referral. Stage 2: offer a separate introduction request once routing is ready. Stage 3: capture service type, location, timing, and explicit permission to share necessary details with the selected provider. Match on actual service area and availability; confirm licensing/insurance checks and describe verification accurately. No guaranteed match and no automatic sale or sharing of uploaded estimates. Disclose referral compensation if applicable. Route an unmatched request to Mark, not an invented provider. Record provider acceptance, homeowner contact, appointment, and outcome. This bridge is planned, not implemented by the offer pages.
