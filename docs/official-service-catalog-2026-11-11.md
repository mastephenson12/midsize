## Implementation rules
- Show starting prices as **From** on Google Business Profiles, and free tools as **Free**.
- Do not put prices in custom Google service names or in the general Google business description.
- Label monthly charges and one-time setup fees explicitly in descriptions, quotes, landing pages, and sales decks.
- MidSize Complete includes FrontDesk, Reputation, Presence, and core follow-up; do not add separate subscription fees for those components to the package price.
- Roofer's Booking Blueprint is a roofing-focused standalone offer, not automatically a surcharge on Complete.
- Price excludes applicable taxes, ad spend, third-party messaging/AI usage overages, major website builds, and out-of-scope integrations. Disclose scope and usage allowances before purchase.
- Do not promise specific lead counts, revenue, ranking results, or 24/7 live human coverage.
- Only publish to legitimate eligible Google Business Profiles. Verify duplicate/overlapping service-area listings before changing them.
- These starting prices are owner-approved. Final terms, usage limits, delivery SLAs, and billing implementation still require operational verification.
