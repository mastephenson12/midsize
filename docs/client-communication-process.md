# Client communication process

Effective: November 18, 2026. Manual operating process; automated delivery is not configured by these website changes.

## Before accepting payment

Mark confirms the service, scope, setup and monthly charges, usage limits, overages, third-party fees, support boundaries, cancellation terms, responsibilities, start date, and change-approval process in writing. Verify integration feasibility before describing it as included and working. The $500 follow-up pilot is coaching and process setup, separate from AI call services.

## Kickoff

Record an owner on each team, the agreed communication channel, baseline, approval requirements, and actual update dates. For the 14-day pilot, arrange the kickoff, midpoint, and closing review. For other services, agree the cadence in the proposal. Do not promise unstaffed response times.

## At every agreed update

1. Read the project record and verify each claimed completed item.
2. List unverified work separately; waiting for approval is not approval.
3. Explain blockers and any proposed scope, price, or timeline changes.
4. Name the next action, its owner, and next update date, including during delays.
5. Use /clients/kickoff/ to prepare a draft if useful. Review and manually send through the agreed channel; the tool sends nothing.
6. Record when the update was sent and retain client decisions in the project record.

Obtain agreement before proceeding with a proposed change. Do not mark delivery or client acceptance based solely on generating a draft.

## Review

Keep inquiries, appointments, booked jobs, booked value, and collected revenue distinct. Disclose missing records and attribution limits. Demos are illustrative. Request honest feedback; do not require positive sentiment, selectively suppress public review invitations, or publish a testimonial without permission.

Website guide: /clients/what-to-expect/. Proposed launch pricing remains a proposal until Mark approves final commercial terms; this change does not alter published prices or billing.
